About me


I’m a Computer Systems Engineering student with a passion for building technology rather than simply learning about it.

My interests span software development, web technologies, databases, networking, embedded systems, cloud infrastructure, and Linux-based systems. I enjoy taking an idea, breaking it down into smaller technical problems, and developing a working solution from the ground up.

One of the projects that has contributed significantly to my practical experience is the development of HOTSHOT, an e-commerce platform that I designed and developed as a real-world web application. Working on HOTSHOT allowed me to go beyond writing frontend and backend code. I worked with Python and Flask for the application backend, HTML, CSS and JavaScript for the user interface, Jinja for dynamic templates, and MySQL for data management.

Building and deploying the platform also exposed me to areas of technology that are often difficult to learn purely from a classroom environment. I gained practical experience with Linux and Ubuntu servers, Nginx, Gunicorn, Cloudflare, DNS configuration, SSL/TLS, domain deployment, environment variables, Git, database management, authentication, payment integration, e-commerce workflows, and troubleshooting production issues.

I have also worked on embedded and IoT projects using ESP32 microcontrollers, sensors, and automation systems. These projects have helped me understand how software interacts with physical hardware and strengthened my skills in programming, electronics, debugging, and system design.

I also enjoy helping other students learn programming and technology. Teaching has strengthened my communication skills while challenging me to understand technical concepts deeply enough to explain them clearly.

I believe that building real projects is one of the most effective ways to become a better technologist. Every project gives me an opportunity to learn something new, solve problems, make mistakes, and improve.

My goal is to continue developing as a technology professional and eventually work on innovative systems where software, Hardware, networking, and cloud technologies come together to solve real-world problems.
